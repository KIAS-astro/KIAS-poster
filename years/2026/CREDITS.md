## SPHEREx mission

**SPHEREx** = *Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of
Reionization, and Ices Explorer* — a NASA infrared space telescope. Launched
2025-03-12 (Falcon 9, Vandenberg), ~650 km Sun-synchronous orbit; it images the
sky ~14.5 orbits/day (~3,600 images/day) and surveys the whole sky every ~6
months in 102 infrared wavelengths.

**Korea's role:** SPHEREx is *jointly developed by the Korea Astronomy and Space
Science Institute (KASI), the National Aeronautics and Space Administration
(NASA), and others.* As an international partner, KASI is involved across all
areas of the project — the development of the calibration facility, the
construction of the data reduction modules, and the science studies for SPHEREx.

The mission is managed by NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory; BAE Systems built the
telescope and spacecraft bus; science is carried out by institutions in the U.S.,
South Korea, and Taiwan, with data processed and archived at IPAC (Caltech).

## All-sky banner (the oval image)

The poster banner is a left/right merge of two SPHEREx all-sky maps released
2025-12-18 (data taken May–December 2025), produced with `cut_oval.py` +
`merge_oval.py`.

Both maps: credit **NASA/JPL-Caltech**, elliptical (Mollweide) projection,
resolution reduced ~1000× from the full SPHEREx data for file size. Invisible
infrared colors are mapped to visible ones — short/medium wavelengths to
blue/green, long wavelengths to red. The bright central band is the Milky Way;
most other points are galaxies.

- **Stars & Galaxies** (StarsRGB), `spherex20251218b` — colors primarily from
  stars and galaxies.
  https://spherex.caltech.edu/image/spherex20251218b-spherex-all-sky-map-2025-stars-and-galaxies
- **Dust & Gas** (LinesRB), `spherex20251218c` — colors primarily from cosmic
  dust (red) and hot gas (blue).
  https://spherex.caltech.edu/image/spherex20251218c-spherex-all-sky-map-2025-dust-and-gas
- AstroPix provider page: https://www.astropix.org/provider/spherex
